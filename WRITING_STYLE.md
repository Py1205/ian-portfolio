# Writing style — human voice

When rewriting or drafting any prose on this site (case studies, project descriptions, hero copy, etc.), follow these rules. Goal: sound like a real person wrote it, not a language model.

## Do

- Use simple language and plain words
- Vary sentence length — mix short punchy lines with longer ones
- Write in active voice
- Show personality (contractions, fragments, the occasional aside are fine)
- Use periods, commas, colons, and parentheses to break up ideas

## Don't

- **No em dashes (—).** Use periods, colons, parens, or commas instead
- **No "A wasn't X. It was Y." mirror-contrast pattern**, or its variants ("not X but Y," "isn't about X, it's about Y"). It's a dead giveaway
- No AI buzzwords: elevate, delve, leverage, unwavering, seamless, robust, foster, harness, testament, navigate, embark, journey
- No empty connectors: furthermore, moreover, in essence, ultimately, that said
- No passive voice when active works
- No padding ("it's worth noting," "it's important to mention," "as we can see")
- Don't repeat what was just said in different words to extend a paragraph

## Process

Before changing any file, list the original and proposed rewrite paragraph by paragraph. Wait for explicit confirmation before applying edits.
